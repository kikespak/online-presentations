Introduction

The Pearl

Lake Ontario drops 245 metres straight down, with no slope for coarse matter to gather on. Its cleansing runs direct from the depths to the surface, which is why the water at the edge of your home behaves like a fountain that keeps on healing.

The boat is the pearl. Small against that water, precious, a bridge between the family and the lake's healing force, and the mark on which that bond can keep growing.

My mission would be to give the pearl its shape. An illustration carried across both amas, and across the sail if budget proves possible, so the symbol acts as a catalyst every time someone steps aboard.

Process

The Weaving

For this project to be truly alive, I’d like to weave a storyline rather than a fixed production pipe. My vision is to include Joshua, Grace, and Anthony in the process through a series of calls, exercises, questions, ponders and even drawings and sketches. It could grow into a complete mini-workshop.

01
TalkA first call to walk through the story, the boat, and the symbol.
02
ListenQuestions and small exercises with Joshua, Grace and Anthony.
03
SketchFirst directions for the amas, and the sail if confirmed, shared for a reaction.
04
RefineOne round of adjustments, decided together.
05
FinishFinal artwork files, prepared and ready to apply.
06
ApplyThe amas and the sail print coordinated once it is a go.
Around two months, held as a storyline rather than a fixed schedule. Timings stay soft, and the door stays open for what the project becomes once the pearl is finished.
